Explore the exciting and mysterious world of wild animals in a natural setting without leaving your home.

Welcome to the Online Zoo!

Welcome to the Online Zoo! On our website, using live webcams, fans of all ages can observe various animals. Among them, are Giant pandas, eagles, alligators, forest gorillas, African lions, and others. It is the whole natural world in real-time in front of our cameras. We hope you will enjoy watching closely and explore animals’ behavior and habitats! Note: animals are not always on view on cameras, so please check back if you don't see anything.

How we work

Online Zoo is a nonprofit committed to inspiring awareness and preservation of nature and wild animals in our zoo and worldwide. Every day, our experts work to safeguard the health and wellness of the animals. To continue these efforts, we need your help. We're so grateful to our numerous supporters. All donations, large and small, go a long way to the conservation efforts of our pets.

meet some our Pets

Do you have a special place in your heart for animals? Who are your favorites? Perhaps you'd like to donate to special ones or all our pets? We think it's important for you to choose how your donation is used.

Giant Panda

Native to central China, giant pandas have come to symbolize vulnerable species.

Lemur

Native to Madagascar, lemurs are highly social primates known for their distinctive tails and vocalizations.

Gorilla

Forest gorillas are intelligent and gentle giants, living in family groups in the rainforests of Central Africa.

Crocodile

Ancient predators of the water, crocodiles are powerful reptiles found in tropical regions worldwide.

Eagle

Majestic birds of prey, eagles are known for their keen vision and powerful hunting abilities.

Koala

Native to Australia, koalas are adorable marsupials that spend most of their lives in eucalyptus trees.

Lion

African lions are powerful carnivores and social creatures, living together in pride groups.

Tiger

Striped apex predators, tigers are solitary hunters and the largest living cats in the world.
